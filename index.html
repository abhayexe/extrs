The problem with paper

A Bahi-Khata can't warn you before the stock runs out.

Every Kirana store runs on trust — the customer who pays next week, the regular who's good for it, the wholesaler who's owed for last month's delivery. For decades, all of that has lived in a cloth-bound register, one page at a time.

BizBabu keeps the same trust, minus the guesswork. It's a digital ledger first — built to replace the Bahi-Khata exactly, Udhaar entry for Udhaar entry — and a business brain second, watching your stock and your customers so you don't have to remember it all.

It's free because a shop that thrives on BizBabu is a shop that keeps using it. No ads, no premium tier, no catch.

The ledger, line by line

Everything your Bahi-Khata did. Plus everything it couldn't.

Four entries. That's the whole app.

01S.No.

The Unified Ledger

Every Udhaar, remembered

GAVE and GOT balance themselves automatically on every bill. Deleted an entry by mistake? A 7-day TimeLock reverses it cleanly — the debt drops and the stock returns to your shelf count.

  • Customer & supplier ledgers, tracked separately
  • Instant Galla profile for walk-in cash sales
02S.No.

Smart POS & Inventory

Scan once, know everything

Point the camera at a barcode. If it's already been scanned by three or more shops on BizBabu, we fill in the product name for you — no typing required. Every sale deducts stock in real time.

  • Crowd-verified global barcode database
  • GSTIN, store logo & custom colours on every bill
03S.No.

Business Intelligence

The shop that thinks ahead

Velocity Brain watches your 7-day sales pace and warns you 3 days before a fast-mover runs dry. Dead-Stock Radar flags anything untouched for 60 days. Every regular customer earns a Trust Score, starting at 75.

  • Overdue collections, surfaced automatically
  • Trust Score rewards early payers, flags 30-day defaults
04S.No.

Security, built in

Guarded like cash in the Galla

Sign in with just your phone number — OTP only, no passwords to lose or leak. Request deletion and every active session logs out globally, instantly, with your data wiped for good after a 20-day safety window.

  • OTP login, verified on every request
  • Instant, global session revocation
Trust, by design

Your shop's data, treated like your shop's cash.

No passwords to steal. No stray sessions left open. Nothing kept longer than it should be.

OTP-only sign-in

Just your phone number. Every critical request is verified server-side before anything moves.

Instant revocation

Ask to delete your account and every device you're logged into is signed out immediately, everywhere.

Data that actually leaves

A 20-day safety window, then a daily process permanently deletes ledgers, bills and profiles for good.
